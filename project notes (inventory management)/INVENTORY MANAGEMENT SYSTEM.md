**INVENTORY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM** for small Business

 

Frontend: UI java swing

Backend: Login java

Database: MySQL or oracle

connector: JDBC

 

**Main Modules**

1.Login Module - Admin Login

2.Product Management - ADD , Update, delete products

3\. Stock Management - Track quantity in/out

4\. supplier Management - Store supplier details

4.Sales Management - Records sold items

5.Reports - View stock and sales reports
